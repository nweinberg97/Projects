## Live and in use

Things people are using now, or that anyone can pick up today.

### NomNoms &nbsp; `Real users`
The baby book that builds itself. Add photos, videos and the occasional note. NomNoms sorts them into chapters, picks the best photos and designs every page, so you get a book to read, share, download or watch as a short film.
**Where it's at:** being tested by a real user. You can look around with a demo family.
[Try it ↗](https://nweinberg97.github.io/NomNoms/) · [Code ↗](https://github.com/nweinberg97/NomNoms)

### Kitside &nbsp; `Anyone can use it`
A local community for Kitsilano. Find interesting people nearby by what they do and what they're building, see what's happening this week, and say "I'm in."
**Where it's at:** real sign-up and a real database, so neighbours can actually join. A demo mode with sample neighbours lets you look around first.
[Try it ↗](https://nweinberg97.github.io/Kitside/) · [Code ↗](https://github.com/nweinberg97/Kitside)

### Sedi &nbsp; `Anyone can use it`
One workspace for tasks, life goals, time and notes, connected by a single card system you drag between tools. Your data stays on your device, and it works offline.
> Grew out of my earlier tools: [Taskly](https://github.com/nweinberg97/taskly), [Boardly](https://github.com/nweinberg97/Boardly), [Timely](https://github.com/nweinberg97/Timely) and [Brainly](https://github.com/nweinberg97/Brainly).

**Where it's at:** fully usable. Some features and visual polish are still being refined.
[Try it ↗](https://nweinberg97.github.io/sedi/) · [Code ↗](https://github.com/nweinberg97/sedi)

### Drift &nbsp; `Anyone can use it`
A quiet focus timer for Mac. Press ⌥ Space from any app, type 25, and get back to work. Voice commands, multiple timers, and no account needed.
**Where it's at:** installs on any Mac with one copy-paste command.
[Get it on GitHub ↗](https://github.com/nweinberg97/Drift)

## Products and prototypes

Real ideas, built as working prototypes you can click through.

### Search for the Good &nbsp; `Demo, sample data`
A search engine for the people, products and projects making the world better. Search in plain language, or keep scrolling a feed of good things. Over 100 real examples from 15 countries.
> Started as LinkedIn posts and interviews, then an early prototype I showed to organizations. Now it's a working, coded product.

[Try it ↗](https://nweinberg97.github.io/Search-for-the-Good/) · [Code ↗](https://github.com/nweinberg97/Search-for-the-Good)

### JumpIn &nbsp; `Demo, sample data`
The planned social side of Search for the Good. A private network where people building a better world can see who's free to talk and jump straight into a video call, without the back and forth of booking a meeting.
[Try it ↗](https://nweinberg97.github.io/JumpIn/) · [Code ↗](https://github.com/nweinberg97/JumpIn)

### FORM &nbsp; `Demo, sample data`
Helps physio patients actually do their home exercises. Patients get one clear session for today, and their physio sees how it went.
**Try:** switch between the patient's app and the physio's dashboard.

### Sogo &nbsp; `Code only`

[Code ↗](https://github.com/nweinberg97/Sogo)

### CoursePack &nbsp; `Demo, sample data`

## Private projects

These live in private repos, so there's no public link. Happy to walk through them on a call.

- **Domino Swarm**: an AI pipeline that finds, checks and researches sales leads each day, so a sales team starts every morning with a list of qualified companies and personalized openers.
- **Learning Loop**: a rep talks for 60 seconds after a sales call, and it turns that into a clean CRM note, spots patterns across the team, and suggests playbook changes for a manager to approve.
- **Growth Signal**: watches companies for signs of fast growth, like hiring sprees, acquisitions and product launches, and ranks who a sales team should call first.
